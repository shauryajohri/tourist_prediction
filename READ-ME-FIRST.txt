SHAANT
Find the calm version of your trip.
================================================================

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
The full version is online (Netlify). Scroll down from the top and
the page drops through the clouds to a still lake, where the search
box is waiting.

Or double-click  index.html  in this folder. Browsers do not let a
double-clicked file load a video, so the opening becomes still
pictures that fade from the clouds to the lake. Everything else works
the same. Nothing to install. No Python. No terminal.

Type a place (spelling does not matter, "massori" finds Mussoorie)
and the page shows, in order:

  1. how crowded it will be on your date
  2. the weather
  3. hotels that fit the budget you drag or type
  4. the places to see there
  5. a map of the drive from where you are

Below that: how to read quiet, normal and very crowded days, three
quieter places to try instead, and answers to common questions.

The crowd, hotels and places work offline. The live weather, the
map and the route need an internet connection. On phones the page
shows a still picture of the lake instead of the scrolling video.


HOW TO SHARE IT WITH SOMEONE
----------------------------
Send them the web link. Or send this whole "shaant" folder, zipped,
and they double-click index.html. Do not send index.html on its
own, it needs the assets folder next to it.

ABOUT THE NUMBERS (please read this one)
----------------------------------------
The crowd level comes from your trained model. The model itself is
fine. The dataset it learned from is sample data, not real visitor
counts.

In that dataset every destination behaves almost identically.
Kedarnath, which is snowed shut for half the year, and Haridwar,
which is a city on the plains open all year, have nearly the same
visitor curve. The gap between destinations is about 3 visitors.
The gap between months is about 52.

So the model reads the SEASON well and cannot read the DESTINATION.
Weekends show no effect at all in the data. The page says so in a
line under the crowd card.

Hotel names and prices are from general knowledge and are rough.
Every hotel card has a Check price link for the real rate.

If you ever get real visitor records, drop them in, retrain with
train_model.py, and re-export. The website itself does not need to
change at all.
